## Approach

I don't start with tools. I start with **questions**:

- What is the actual problem we're solving?
- What are the real constraints (power, cost, latency, maintainability)?
- What happens when something fails in the field?

Good architecture is invisible. It works, it's maintainable, and it doesn't over-engineer.
